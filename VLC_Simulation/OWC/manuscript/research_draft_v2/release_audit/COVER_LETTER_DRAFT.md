# Cover letter — guide to finalize before submission

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, “Fixed and Joint Optical RIS–Friendly Jamming Control for Secure Visible Light Communication: A Matched Training-Budget Study.”

The paper examines whether expanding a discrete friendly-jamming action space with an optical RIS codebook improves a CNN/differentiable-neural-dictionary controller under matched training budgets. It compares learned policies with the best available finite-grid actions, reports seed-level uncertainty, and separates the inherited paper-equation metrics from a current-domain finite-alphabet consistency diagnostic. The contribution is this bounded comparison and its reproducibility evidence, rather than a claim that optical RIS and deep reinforcement learning have not previously been combined.

The paper may interest readers studying optical-wireless physical-layer security and the evaluation of learning-based control. The accompanying package records the implemented model conventions, run data, analysis procedures and test outputs. Conclusions are restricted to the stated geometry/codebook and evaluation conditions; no hardware security or general joint-controller superiority is claimed.

Before submitting, the corresponding author must add the chosen journal, final manuscript title if changed, correspondence details, and the journal's required declarations on exclusivity, author approval, funding, competing interests and AI assistance. Those declarations are not asserted by this draft.

Sincerely,

The corresponding author, on behalf of the four student authors
