6 · Physical Boundaries, Assumptions & Supervisor Discussion Guide

Explicit Simulation Assumptions

  • Direct-Current (DC) Optical Channel: Single-bounce specular mirror reflection using image-source virtual emitters. Dispersion, multipath delay spread, and ISI are omitted under the low-bandwidth VLC regime.
  • Oracle Channel State Information (CSI): Bob and Eve coordinates are assumed known in the simulation environment to generate codebooks.
  • Omitted RIS Actuation Power: The power required to mechanically tilt mirrors or switch states is not counted in the utility function.
  • Energy Interpretation: Primary results use nonnegative L1 norm ∑|wi|; printed paper equation uses signed sum ∑wi.

Boundary Conditions & Honest Disclosures

  • Near-Bob Boundary (dBE = 7.07 cm): When Eve is spatially collinear between Alice and Bob (hAE > hAB), exhaustive search across all 3,125 actions yields max Rs ≡ 0.0000 bps/Hz. Security cannot be established purely via friendly jamming in this geometric singularity.
  • Colocated Boundary: When Eve is identical to Bob, channel gains are indistinguishable and secrecy is identically zero.
  • 2024 Paper Numerical Non-Reproduction: The published curves (Rs ≈ 5.68 bps/Hz) cannot be verified with the paper's declared physical constants (under which single-channel capacity ≈ 1.55 bps/Hz).
